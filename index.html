Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to incorporate a US or UK company?

For the US: passport copy, proposed company name, registered agent address, and details of members/directors. For the UK: passport copy, proposed name, director and shareholder details, and a UK registered office address. The entire process can be done online with video call verification.

Can a foreigner be the sole director and shareholder?

Yes. Both US LLCs/Corporations and UK limited companies can have a single individual who is also the sole shareholder/member. Neither requires the director to be a local resident.

How long does it take to incorporate a company?

US: 1–3 business days (Delaware or Wyoming). UK: as fast as 24 hours via Companies House e-incorporation. Expedited same-day service is available for both.

What is the minimum share capital?

US: as low as $1 in Delaware and Wyoming. UK: a minimum of £1 (one share at £1). There is no upper limit for either jurisdiction.

Do I need to be in the US or UK to open a bank account?

No. We support fully online business accounts with US providers (Mercury, Brex, Wise) and UK providers (Revolut, Tide, Starling). No in-person visit required.

When are my tax returns due?

US: LLCs (Schedule C) file with your personal return by April 15; Corporations (Form 1120) by March 15. UK: Self Assessment by January 31; Corporation Tax (CT600) within 12 months of the accounting period end.

What are the ongoing compliance requirements?

US LLCs file an annual report (varies by state) and BOI report. UK companies file a confirmation statement (CS01) annually and accounts within 9 months of the accounting period end.

Do you offer Chinese-language services?

Yes. Our team is fluent in English, Mandarin (Simplified & Traditional), and Cantonese. All incorporation documents, statutory forms, and correspondence can be prepared in both English and Chinese.
